# ADR 0001. Vibe Engineering Bible 2.0

- **Status:** accepted 2026-10-08 (release 2.0).
- **Date:** 2026-10-08.
- **Process context.** The core (§4, §4½, §5) moves only through the Appendix E gate — a reasoned ADR, no more than once a quarter. Version 2.0 is a scheduled revision of the core based on external evidence from 2025–2026, not a promotion of field patterns. That is why there are no new sins: the candidates went into the `patterns.md` staging queue and wait for ≥3 independent confirmations.
- **Basis:** decisions of the bible owners of 2026-10-08 (tone register, process layer, consequences, name, owner group), recorded in this ADR; sources — Appendix D of the bible (the "version 2.0" groups); a process layer contributed in June 2026.

## Decisions

### 1. Two covenants = two modes; T1–T3 are risk tiers (§2, §3, §4 II)
- **Context.** Four equal "zones" read as four modes and bred the argument "is this still a prototype or already a product". The bible itself already defined the T0 → T1 transition by events.
- **Decision.** There are two modes: Freedom (T0) and Discipline (T1+). T1/T2/T3 are a risk layer inside Discipline. The transition out of Freedom is event-triggered (shown outside the team, used by someone else, real data/secrets/shared state needed, asked for it on the roadmap). The trigger "started using it daily yourself" is removed — it does not concern other people. Agent autonomy by tier is added.
- **Consequences.** The T0–T3 labels and DoD anchors are kept; Commandment II is renamed ("Freedom and Discipline are two different codes"). Mapping tiers onto a specific organization's autonomy ladder stays out of the core.

### 2. The law lives in the environment (§4 IV, §8)
- **Context.** ETH (arXiv 2602.11988): context files do not raise task success and make work >20% more expensive. Companies that scaled agents (Stripe, Spotify — self-reports) enforce rules through the environment: isolated environments, a tool catalog, deterministic checks.
- **Decision.** Commandment IV "One stack, one paved road" → "The law lives in the environment" (the paved road remains part of it). §8: context files hold only the non-obvious (~150 lines), with an explicit split "what the environment enforces / what the file suggests"; "emphasis works" is softened to "emphasis is for what cannot be moved into the environment".
- **Consequences.** The CLAUDE.md template is rewritten shorter; DoD row #36.

### 3. Agent configuration is a supply chain (§4 V)
- **Context.** Liu et al. (arXiv 2601.10338): 26.1% of marketplace skills are vulnerable, skills with scripts are 2.12 times riskier; MCP incidents of 2025.
- **Decision.** Commandment V is extended: skills, plugins, MCP, hooks, AGENTS.md are dependencies with owners, review, and pinning; anything external goes through an internal catalog.
- **Consequences.** DoD #35; §7 checklist item 9; candidate `#unvetted-skill` — in staging.

### 4. Review: AI advises, a human decides; human review capacity is a resource (§4 VII, §4½ #12)
- **Context.** In all documented large deployments AI review is advisory (Uber uReview, Atlassian RovoDev). Self-attribution bias (arXiv 2603.04582). DORA 2025: the bottleneck is human review; vendor data on growing review time.
- **Decision.** Commandment VII is extended; `#sycophancy` is clarified as "AI checks AI without a human, including the agent's self-review"; auto-merge for T1–T2 only after the checks are calibrated, for T3 — never.
- **Consequences.** DoD #37; capacity metrics in §9.

### 5. Done means proven; the loop up to the outcome (§4 VIII, §6)
- **Context.** Work was "closed" by a merge; the link to production (rollout, metrics, experiments) existed in the bible only in T3 rows. Analysts and agentic systems were not described.
- **Decision.** Commandment VIII → "Done means proven": an evidence bundle + the outcome. §6 → the loop "spec → agent → verification → rollout → outcome", a spec sized to the task (caveat: SDD is in "Assess" on the Thoughtworks Radar), lifecycle +Verified, a "Not only code" section (the analyst is the author of an executable spec; evals for AI products).
- **Consequences.** DoD #38, #39; checklist item 10; candidate `#metric-free` — in staging.

### 6. Consequences of sins without punishing people (§4½)
- **Context.** "Incident report", "escalation to a policy owner", "access-level review", "a conversation with the tech lead" turned the rejection vocabulary into a disciplinary mechanism; adoption research: a champion must not be a police officer; adopter feedback: the framework must not stifle the work.
- **Decision.** A mortal sin = the PR is not merged until fixed, with a blameless review (what in the environment allowed the mistake to happen). A venial sin = back for rework; a recurring pattern is a reason to fix the process and the environment, not the person. The names and the count "thirteen" do not change.

### 7. Two gate classes (§5, §9)
- **Context.** The universal "advisory → hard gate, never the other way round" postponed protection where a check is exact and cheap (secrets, slopsquatting, SAST), while being right for probabilistic checks.
- **Decision.** A "Gate" column in DoD: D — deterministic, blocks immediately; P — probabilistic, "advisory → calibration → block" (calibration against humans, target TPR/TNR > 90%); H — human decision. Rows #35–#39.

### 8. Metrics in pairs; owner group (§9)
- **Context.** A single speed metric grows at the expense of quality (DORA 2025). A mandatory central policy body read as a committee.
- **Decision.** The main KPI is two pairs: speed + share of rework/rollbacks; verification before rollout + waiting time for review and PR size; adoption is tracked separately. Ownership is an owner group by parts + a harness owner; a large organization may call the owner group a guild. "Infrastructure first, new access later".

### 9. Process layer (Appendix F)
- **Context.** A contributed process layer added the application modes init / audit / review / proposal, playbooks, and report templates; its "Appendix E. Application" conflicted with "Appendix E. Standard evolution".
- **Decision.** Application is a new Appendix F; the playbooks are the first development-agent skills; the adoption registry is merged into `registry.md`. The principle "audit first, then gate" is reconciled with the two gate classes: an existing project starts with an audit, deterministic gates for new changes are on immediately.

### 10. Name and tone
- **Decision.** A single name — "Vibe Engineering Bible" (the H1 used to be "The Vibe Coding Bible"). The sacral register stays; a team that needs a secular register translates it locally (norm / antipattern / prohibition) without changing the meaning. *Inquisitio Codicis* is a rite of checking code, not people.

## Deliberately not changed
- The counts: two covenants, eight commandments, thirteen sins.
- Section numbering and DoD anchors (new rows — #35–#39).
- The figures in §1 and §4½ — except the caveat to METR's "−19%" (METR itself marked the result as unreliable in its 2026 update).
